# Q2(d) — Threat agents, controls, and regulatory framing

## Scope and legal framing

GDPR is used as the assignment's reference framework. Its territorial applicability is subject to Article 3 and is not automatic merely because personal data is processed. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 provides the domestic legal context. This analysis does not claim legal compliance.

Resumes are personal data because they can contain a person's name, contact details, education, work history, skills, and assessment information. The assignment prescribes GDPR as its personal-data reference framework. GDPR Article 3 can apply where EU data subjects are involved, for example international applicants; for an India-based campus portal, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is the domestic context and also requires reasonable security safeguards.
